Object Manipulator (Magnet) in Palworld Explained

August 6, 2026

Object Manipulator (Magnet) pulls items, resources, and objects toward your character. It is one of the highest-value QoL modules for Palworld crafting hacks and resource loops.

Controls

  • Field of View — capture area around the player
  • Filters — choose item and resource categories
  • Magnet — automatic pull inside FOV
  • Key — hotkey to toggle the manipulator

Resources and Eggs

Yes — magnet filters can include resources and eggs. Spot them with Items ESP, then pull what you need without walking every node.

Recommended Flow

  1. Enable Items ESP filters for your current goal
  2. Set magnet FOV conservatively
  3. Bind a hotkey and toggle only when farming
  4. Use Lightweight Backpack so inventory weight does not stop the loop
